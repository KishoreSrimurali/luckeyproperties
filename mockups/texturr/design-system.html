Rules

Colour

  • Bronze and Stone never set text under 24px on bone.
  • Only one dark band per screen, plus the footer.
  • Alpha is for light, shadow and texture only, never to invent a flat UI colour.
  • Errors are signalled with weight, icon and copy, never with a new red.

Motion and interaction

  • One entrance per page: text rises 14px over 700ms, imagery unveils over 1s. Nothing loops.
  • Hover changes are 200–350ms colour or underline only.
  • Every animation is removed under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Tap targets ≥ 44px. Focus ring: 2px Bronze, 3px offset (Sand on dark).
